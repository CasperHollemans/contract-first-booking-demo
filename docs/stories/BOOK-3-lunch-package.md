# BOOK-3 · Lunch package upsell
Status: draft

**As a** booker
**I want** to add a lunch package to my booking at a discount
**So that** _(to confirm)_

## Acceptance criteria
- The lunch package is only available for bookings of 1 hour or longer.
- The booker chooses how many lunches.
- Lunch is priced per person at a discounted rate.
- The booker is shown how much they save, e.g. "You save x% on your lunch".
- If the number of lunches is higher than the number of people, the booker must confirm this in a confirmation modal.
- A booking can include at most 8 lunches. For more than 8, the booker is shown a message to contact the cafe.

## Open questions
- What is the "so that" for this story?
- What is the lunch price and the discount?
- What does the lunch consist of? Are there menu choices?
- When is lunch served during the booking?
- Can lunch be added after the booking is made?
