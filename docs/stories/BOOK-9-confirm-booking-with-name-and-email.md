# BOOK-9 · Confirm a booking with name and email
Status: approved

**As a** user
**I want** to confirm my booking by entering my name and email
**So that** I can make use of the room for a prespecified time period

## Acceptance criteria
- The user fills in their name and email to confirm the booking.
- The booking is made at the moment the user confirms it.
- After confirming, the user sees a simple thank-you page.

## Out of scope (demo MVP)
- Payment.
- Anything beyond a simple thank-you page (e.g. confirmation email).
